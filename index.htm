Guilherme Ozorio Cassol

Lab Technician,
Computer Process Control Lab,
University of Alberta

PhD in Chemical Engineering (University of Alberta, 2022). I design, instrument, and automate chemical engineering laboratories; mentor TAs; and help deliver hands‑on lab experiences across CPC courses.

My research spans distributed parameter systems (PDE‑based modelling and control), optimal/MPC, real‑time estimation, and SCADA/HMI implementations with PLCs, microcontrollers and web back‑ends. Recent work includes algae growth monitoring, natural circulation loops, phase‑change systems and rare minerals recovery.


Contact: ozorioca@ualberta.ca

Research

Research

  • Modelling, experimental planning, and data acquisition for batch and continuous adsorption processes.
  • Modelling, data acquisition, and optimization of enzymatic hydrolysis for bioethanol production.
  • Experimental design of a continuous crystallizer with recycle and a cooling jacket.
  • Development of an autonomous sailing vessel.
  • Development and control of a small‑scale natural circulation loop for energy studies.
  • Cultivation, sensing, and monitoring of algae growth.
  • Development and control of small‑scale phase‑change systems with moving boundaries.
  • Real‑time implementation of advanced control and estimation, including SCADA/HMI prototypes with Arduino or ESP32 and web back‑ends.
